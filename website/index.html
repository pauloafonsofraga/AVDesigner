About / Paulo Fraga

Video engineering first.
Software with a purpose.

VideoCore is my professional brand and the home of the AV software I build.

My background is in professional video engineering. Working with real AV systems is what inspires these tools: the signal paths that need to be understood, the technical details that need to stay consistent and the decisions that need to become clear before a production.

WireNexus brings that perspective to system design and documentation. LEDForge focuses on LED screen layouts, calculations and visual previews. Different tools, connected by the workflows that inspired them.
