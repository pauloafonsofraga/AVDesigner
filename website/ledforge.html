Inside LEDForge

The screen and its details,
in the same workflow.

01 / LAYOUT

Start with the panels.

Choose panel systems, arrange screen layouts and work with rows, columns and physical dimensions. Screen and Grid views support the planning workflow.

02 / CALCULATIONS

Keep the numbers in view.

Review panel counts, resolution, physical size, weight and power calculations using the configured panel data. Results depend on the specifications entered and require engineering review.

03 / 3D DIMENSIONS

Look beyond the flat layout.

Explore screen assemblies and dimensional overlays in 3D. Supported configurations include curved-screen workflows; complex assemblies remain subject to geometry and panel-data limitations.

04 / PANEL SYSTEMS

Work with panel definitions.

Use the panel catalogue and editor for panel specifications, variants and compatible locks. Save and reopen local projects as the design develops.
