Good to know

Plans, without surprises.

Can I create an account now?

Public registration is not open. Existing provisioned users can sign in with their current account. This website does not create accounts or change access permissions.

Where can I see my actual limits?

Open WireNexus and select your plan from the projects dashboard. Plans & Usage displays the server-reported entitlements, usage and limits for your authorized workspaces.

Is Enterprise live collaboration generally available?

No. It is implemented and being validated through controlled private staging tests. Broader availability has not been announced.

What happens to local projects when my plan changes?

Local .avd files and local exports remain available. Cloud downgrades preserve existing project data, but new writes may be limited by the workspace's quotas.
